### [CAGE-1: Control, Assurance, and Governance Evaluation for Enterprise Agentic AI](./publications/cage-1/) Roopam W. Sure · Independent technical report · July 2026 CAGE-1 introduces an evaluation framework for enterprise agentic AI. It assesses whether governed agents are ready for deployment across authority, policy enforcement, retrieval trust, memory integrity, tool safety, auditability, human oversight, conflict handling, safe failure, operational readiness, business fitness, and Prebind Assurance.
