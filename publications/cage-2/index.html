Abstract

Autonomous AI systems increasingly act on external systems rather than merely produce recommendations. They can modify access rights, release payments, change infrastructure, delete records, invoke enterprise services, and initiate other operations with durable business consequences.

Existing identity, authorization, policy, and runtime-control mechanisms can determine whether an agent or request may proceed, but those controls do not by themselves establish what ultimately became true in the external system.

CAGE-2 introduces Consequence Assurance, an architectural model for preserving assurance across the transition from an autonomous decision to an externally verified business effect. It extends the Prebind Assurance model introduced in CAGE-1 and subsequently implemented in CAGE-lite.

The central invariant is:

Decision ≠ Effect

CAGE-2 introduces stable Consequence identity, separates Evaluation Attempts from ExecutionAttempts, formalizes Consequence Custody for controlled external execution, separates execution observations from authoritative verification, represents unresolved outcomes through EFFECT_UNKNOWN, and preserves assurance history through DecisionProof, EffectProof, and Warrants.

Research Contribution

CAGE-2 develops a consequence-centric assurance model for autonomous systems in which permission, execution, observation, verification, and externally evidenced effect remain distinct.

The work introduces Consequence Custody across the external execution boundary and an assurance-lineage model based on DecisionProof, EffectProof, and Warrants.

A system may know what it permitted. It may know what it attempted. Only evidence can establish what became true.

Reference Implementation

The architecture is implemented in the open-source cage-assurance Python SDK.

The evaluated release is cage-assurance==0.7.1.

The reference implementation includes guarded execution, Consequence Custody, explicit uncertainty handling, verification, reconciliation, consequence-level idempotency, and Warrant lineage.
